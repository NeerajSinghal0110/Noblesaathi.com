Before you begin

A few helpful answers.

Can I submit biodata for a family member?

Yes, with that person's permission. Mention your relationship and let us know who should handle the enquiry.

Will my WhatsApp biodata appear automatically on the website?

No. Sending a biodata or posting in a WhatsApp group does not publish it here. Noble Saathi must review it and obtain permission before adding a public profile.
